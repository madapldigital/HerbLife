01

The situation

Cannabis clubs and dispensaries can't run the ads most retail businesses rely on. That puts more weight on the moments already within your control: WiFi at the counter, the visitor's first tap-in, and what people say about you online afterwards.

75% of consumers say they regularly or always read online reviews before choosing a local business, and 88% say they'd use a business that responds to all of its reviews, according to BrightLocal's 2024 Local Consumer Review Survey. A Harvard Business School study of Yelp ratings found a one-star increase is linked to a 5 to 9% revenue lift.

Right now, most of that runs on paper and memory: a handwritten WiFi password, no easy way to prompt a Google review while the visit is still fresh.

02

What ReviewTap does

NFC tags placed around your space, each opening a specific hosted page the moment a phone taps it. No app for visitors to download. Industry estimates put NFC completion at 60 to 80% at the point of service, against 35 to 50% for QR codes, since a tap is one action against several for a scan.

Guest WiFi

Network and password ready to copy, no handwritten card at the till.

Visit Your Website

A tap sends visitors straight to your site. Businesses with a website are seen as 41% more trustworthy than those without one, per DreamHost's 2026 Local Business Trust Index.

Google Reviews

A tap at checkout sends satisfied customers straight to your review form while the visit is still fresh.
