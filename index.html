01

The situation

Herb Life runs four stores across Durban. Like any cannabis retailer, it can't run the paid ads a normal retail brand would use to grow, so word of mouth and Google reviews carry more of that weight.

75% of consumers say they regularly or always read online reviews before choosing a local business, and 88% say they'd use a business that responds to all of its reviews, versus 47% for one that never responds, according to BrightLocal's 2024 Local Consumer Review Survey. A Harvard Business School study of Yelp ratings found that a one-star increase is linked to a 5 to 9% increase in revenue.

Meanwhile, day-to-day basics like WiFi at the counter still run on paper and memory, with no easy way to prompt a review while the visit is still fresh.

02

What ReviewTap does for Herb Life

NFC tags around the Hillcrest store, each opening a hosted page the moment a phone taps it. No app to download. Industry estimates put NFC completion at 60 to 80% at the point of service, against 35 to 50% for QR codes, since a tap is one action where a QR code needs someone to open a camera, scan, wait, and confirm.

Guest WiFi

Network and password ready to copy at the counter, no handwritten card.

Visit Our Website

A tap sends visitors straight to herblifesa.co.za. Businesses with a website are seen as 41% more trustworthy than those without one, per DreamHost's 2026 Local Business Trust Index.

Google Reviews

A tap at checkout sends happy customers straight to Herb Life's Google review form, while the visit is still fresh in mind.

Investment

Pricing is tailored to how many tags you need and which extras you want (menu page, Google Business Profile work, ongoing reporting). Rather than list every combination here, tell us what you're interested in and we'll size a quote around it.

Monthly maintenance is capped per store and includes one included call-out a month for wording or content changes. Further changes within the same month are billed separately.
